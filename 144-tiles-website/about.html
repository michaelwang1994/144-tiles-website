Our Story

About us

What started as a memory, quickly became an idea. We hosted one house party and then became the biggest mahjong group in Chicago.

We embraced our whimsy, our stories and our heritage.

144 Tiles is run by Michael Wang and Justice Chow. We focus on Hong Kong and Filipino styles of mahjong and are comfortable hosting large 100+ person events as well as intimate gatherings. We offer lessons for beginners as well as guided play for anyone who needs a refresher.
