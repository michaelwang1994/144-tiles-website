Our mission

We exist to reclaim and celebrate the stories behind mahjong, honoring its cultural roots while building a modern, inclusive community around it.

Through teaching traditional styles of mahjong, we aim to make the game accessible, engaging, and meaningful for everyone, from first-timers to lifelong players.

At 144 Tiles, we create spaces where people can show up as they are, connect across generations and backgrounds, and experience the joy, strategy, and cultural depth of the game together.
